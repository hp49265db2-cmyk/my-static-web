2. Production Line Retrofit & Automation Adaptation

Core Service
Giving New Life to Old Production Lines

For clients with existing legacy production lines, we provide line retrofit and automation adaptation support. Through in-depth evaluation of existing equipment and processes, we develop practical retrofit plans to help clients achieve line upgrades with relatively low investment.

Seamless Integration with Your Vision Inspection System

Our retrofit solutions place special emphasis on compatibility with video inspection systems. Through optimized workstation layouts, data interface design and control system integration, we ensure the retrofitted line works efficiently with your inspection system for automated quality control.

Modular Retrofitting, Flexible & Expandable

We adopt a modular retrofitting approach, supporting phased implementation and gradual upgrades to avoid large one-time investments. Our plans balance current needs with future expansion, leaving room for continuous line improvement.

  • Legacy line assessment & retrofit planning
  • Vision inspection system integration

  • Automation equipment installation & commissioning
  • Modular phased implementation
  • Low-cost production line upgrades

3. Small-Batch Trial Production & Flexible Manufacturing

Core Service
Solving the Three Major R&D Challenges

Addressing the common R&D phase challenges of "small quantities, frequent design changes and high costs", we provide small-batch trial production and flexible manufacturing services to help clients complete product validation and iteration with flexible capacity and controlled costs.

Fast Iteration, Flexible Response

Through modular process design and quick changeover workflows, we efficiently respond to client design changes and version iterations. Without the high investment of traditional tooling, we enable flexible trial production across multiple batches and small volumes.

Reducing R&D Trial Costs

Our flexible manufacturing model effectively reduces pre-launch R&D and trial costs, helping clients complete more validation and optimization cycles within limited budgets, accelerating the journey from development to mass production.
