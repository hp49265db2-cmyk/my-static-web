1. Automotive Parts — 100% In-Line Dimension Inspection

Automotive
Challenge

A Tier-1 automotive supplier needed 100% in-line inspection of critical dimensions and surface defects on engine cylinder blocks. The original manual sampling method had a high missed-detection rate (3.5%) and could not meet the 1.2-second production takt time.

Solution

We designed and integrated a customized non-standard vision inspection system based on deep learning. The system uses 4 high-resolution industrial cameras with custom telecentric lenses and ring lights to capture images from multiple angles. Our self-developed AI algorithms complete dimension measurement and defect recognition within 1.2 seconds, automatically rejecting defective parts through PLC linkage.

Results
  • Accuracy: ±0.02mm
  • Cycle time: 1.2s per part
  • Missed-detection: 3.5% → <0.1%
  • Automation rate: 95%
  • Deep learning-based AI inspection
  • Multi-angle high-res camera array
  • PLC integration for auto rejection
  • Annual labor savings: ¥1.2M

2. Food Packaging — Seal Integrity Inspection

Food
Challenge

A food processing company exporting to Europe relied on manual inspection for the seal integrity of nitrogen-flushed packaging. The process was inefficient, lacked traceable quality records, and led to multiple customer complaints due to poor sealing.

Solution

We deployed an intelligent vision inspection system that integrates thermal imaging and visible-light cameras. The system analyzes temperature distribution and contour features in the sealing area to judge heat-seal quality. It automatically records inspection data for each product and generates traceable reports.

Results
  • Speed: 180 packages/min
  • Accuracy: 99.6%
  • Complaint rate ↓90%
  • Full traceability achieved
  • Thermal + visible light fusion
  • Automatic data recording & reporting
  • Passed EU customer factory audit
  • Non-destructive inspection

3. Electronics — Connector Pin Defect Detection

Electronics
Challenge

A precision electronics manufacturer faced quality issues with pin connectors. Defects such as bent pins, missing pins, and height inconsistencies were difficult for traditional CCD systems to detect reliably, resulting in multiple returns from end customers (800ppm outgoing defect rate).

Solution

We implemented a multi-angle high-magnification microscopic imaging solution, combined with our proprietary sub-pixel edge localization algorithm and 3D height measurement technology. The system precisely inspects the position, height, and coplanarity of each pin. It supports 12 different connector models with a changeover time of just 5 minutes.

Results
  • Precision: 0.01mm
  • 100% pin coverage
  • Defect rate: 800ppm → <50ppm
  • Changeover: 5 minutes
  • Sub-pixel edge localization
  • 3D height measurement
  • Supports 12 connector models
  • Fast changeover, low idle cost

4. Pharmaceutical — Remote Delivery for Blister Packaging

Pharmaceutical
Challenge

A mid-sized pharmaceutical manufacturer in Australia wanted to upgrade its blister packaging line. However, with no factory presence in China and no in-house vision expertise, they struggled to find a suitable non-standard automation supplier.

Solution

We adopted a remote collaboration model. In the initial phase, we designed the solution using samples and video materials provided by the customer. Equipment manufacturing and debugging were completed in China, then shipped to Australia in modular form. We provided remote video guidance for installation and worked with local electricians for final integration — all without the need for our personnel to travel overseas.

Results
  • Delivery: 45 days (contract to production)
  • Inspects: missing pills, breaks, foreign objects, printing
  • Saved ¥300K in travel & on-site costs
  • Long-term remote partnership established
  • End-to-end remote collaboration
  • Modular design for easy shipping
  • Video-guided installation
  • Zero on-site travel required
